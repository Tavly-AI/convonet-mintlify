If you instead require contact synchronization from HubSpot into Tavly or bidirectional timeline activity logging, configure the [HubSpot CRM integration](/integrations/crm/hubspot/connect). Both integrations operate independently and can be deployed concurrently.

## What is the Tavly AI HubSpot app and how does it automate outbound calls?

**Tavly AI HubSpot application** integrates autonomous voice agents into HubSpot automation workflows, introducing the native **Make a Phone Call** workflow action. This capability enables marketing and sales teams to automatically trigger AI-driven phone calls upon lead generation or deal stage movements, write call logs, and log structured call analyses back to CRM records.

The application registers an integrated workflow action inside HubSpot. When a contact reaches the **Make a Phone Call** action step, HubSpot initiates an outbound call via Tavly's telephony network and pauses workflow progression until the conversation concludes.

### Automated post-call CRM synchronization

Upon call completion, Tavly updates HubSpot CRM records automatically:
* **Contact Activity Timeline**: Automatically writes call summaries, duration metrics, and [Post Call Extraction](/monitor/post-call-analysis-overview) data.
* **Native Call Log**: Attaches the full audio recording, caller metadata, and timestamped conversational transcript.
* **Associated Company Timeline**: Automatically associates the call record with the contact's primary company record.

## How do you build a HubSpot workflow to trigger automated AI phone calls?

In HubSpot, navigate to **Automation → Workflows** and create a workflow triggered by events like form submissions or record creation. Add an enrollment filter confirming **Phone number is known**, insert the **Make a Phone Call** integrated action, select your agent, map contact phone properties, and pass dynamic JSON context variables.

### Primary trigger use cases

| Trigger Use Case | Operational Scenario | Business Value |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Instant Lead Qualification** | Contact submits high-intent demo form | Qualifies lead within seconds, reducing response latency. |
| **New Contact Enrollment** | Contact created from inbound campaign | Engages new CRM entries automatically. |
| **Deal Stage Acceleration** | Deal moves to "Proposal Sent" | Proactively calls decision-maker to address open objections. |
| **Stale Lead Re-engagement** | Contact inactivity exceeds 60 days | Re-evaluates interest via low-pressure conversational check-in. |
| **Appointment Confirmation** | Meeting booked 24 hours in advance | Confirms attendance and collects preliminary meeting agenda. |

<Note>
  Operational requirement: Your Tavly AI workspace must have at least one configured conversational voice agent provisioned with an active outbound phone number.
</Note>

## Frequently asked questions about triggering Tavly calls from HubSpot

The Tavly AI HubSpot app enables HubSpot workflows to trigger outbound voice calls autonomously. This works independently of the two-way **HubSpot CRM integration**, which syncs contact directories. Workflows pause while calls execute, allowing downstream conditional branching based on call success, detected voicemail, or custom extraction variables.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does the workflow action wait for the phone call to complete?">
    Yes. The **Make a Phone Call** workflow action temporarily suspends workflow progression for that enrolled contact until the voice call finishes. This architecture ensures all call outcomes, sentiment scores, and extracted variables are populated in CRM memory before downstream workflow actions execute.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I pass personalized dynamic variables into the call?">
    Yes. The **Dynamic Variables** input on the workflow action accepts JSON-formatted key-value pairs (e.g., `{"contact_name": "[Contact: First Name]"}`). These variables are passed into the agent's conversational prompt at runtime, enabling tailored greetings and context-aware responses.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I deploy both the HubSpot Marketplace app and the HubSpot CRM integration?">
    Yes. The two integrations serve complementary roles:
    * The **Marketplace App** allows HubSpot workflows to trigger outbound calls and receive immediate post-call updates.
    * The **CRM Integration** enables continuous bidirectional contact synchronization and agent functions within Tavly.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if a contact does not have a phone number?">
    To prevent workflow execution errors, always insert an enrollment condition specifying **Phone number is known** before the call action. Contacts missing phone numbers will skip the action without causing failed workflow tasks.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
