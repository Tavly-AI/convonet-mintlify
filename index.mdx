## WHAT IS TAVLY AI?

## Build

Tavly AI gives your product programmable voice agents for outbound calls, inbound calls, web calls, bulk campaigns, and post-call analytics. Create the assistant in the dashboard, trigger calls from your backend, and receive results through webhooks or the call history APIs.

Tavly agents are node-based flows or single prompts, with call handling, a knowledge base, and integrations built in. See the full [**build overview**](https://docs.tavlyai.com/build/overview) for everything you can configure, starting with your agent type.
