## What is Tavly AI's commitment to reliability?

**Tavly AI guarantees >99.9% uptime** by making reliability our primary operational priority across all voice AI interactions. We back this commitment through **enterprise-grade infrastructure**, **automated fallbacks and resilience mechanisms**, comprehensive **24/7 telemetry monitoring and alerting**, and dedicated **around-the-clock technical support** for mission-critical conversational deployments.

Our operational model is designed to withstand traffic spikes, carrier degradations, and upstream AI vendor latency shifts. We invest continuously across four core pillars:

1. **Enterprise-Grade Infrastructure**: Scalable compute, redundant networking, and self-hosted model isolation.
2. **Fallbacks and Resilience Features**: Automated multi-tier failover routing for LLM, TTS, and telephony layers.
3. **24/7 Monitoring and Alerting**: Proactive, sub-second telemetry auditing across end-to-end conversational pipelines.
4. **24/7 Technical Support**: Around-the-clock on-call engineering response with guaranteed SLAs.

## What key areas determine voice AI service quality?

Tavly AI maintains exceptional service quality by targeting three foundational pillars: **phone call performance** ensuring consistent inbound and outbound connectivity, **agent reliability** delivering deterministic low-latency audio processing during live interactions, and **agent performance** guaranteeing highly accurate speech transcription alongside strict, reliable adherence to custom prompt instructions.

| Service Quality Pillar | Core Operational Focus | Technical Guarantee |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Phone Call Performance** | Inbound/outbound call orchestration, network stability, and carrier connectivity | High voice quality, persistent connections throughout conversations, and zero unexpected drops |
| **Agent Reliability** | Conversational flow stability and round-trip execution | Deterministic, ultra-low latency during real-time human-agent audio interactions |
| **Agent Performance** | Speech processing and instruction execution | Accurate automated speech recognition (ASR) transcription and strict adherence to prompt instructions |

### 1. Phone Call Performance
* **Inbound & Outbound Handling**: Dependable routing and connection initiation across worldwide public switched telephone networks (PSTN) and SIP trunks.
* **Persistent Connections**: Uninterrupted audio streaming throughout lengthy multi-turn conversations.
* **High Voice Quality**: Uncompressed, crystal-clear telephone audio with minimal packet jitter and artifact suppression.

### 2. Agent Reliability
* **Consistent Low Latency**: Stable round-trip audio delivery ensuring interactions feel natural, fluid, and human-paced without awkward pauses.

### 3. Agent Performance
* **Accurate Speech Transcription**: High-precision speech-to-text decoding across diverse accents, background noise environments, and domain vocabularies.
* **Strict Prompt Adherence**: Deterministic execution of system prompts, safety guardrails, dynamic variables, and custom function-calling routines.

## How does Tavly AI proactively monitor platform telemetry?

Tavly AI operates **24/7 proactive latency monitoring and automated alerting** to detect and remediate anomalies before they impact production workloads. Systems continuously track **p75, p90, p95, and p99 latency distributions** across ASR, LLM, and TTS pipelines, alongside system resource consumption, API response times, and failure rates.

| Telemetry Domain | Monitored Metrics & KPIs | Operational Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Latency Distributions** | ASR, TTS, LLM, Knowledge Base, TTFT; p75, p90, p95, p99 percentiles | Prevents conversational lag and ensures sub-second conversational pacing |
| **Pipeline Reliability** | Failed call counts, ASR/TTS/LLM timeouts, provider error rates | Immediately isolates failing upstream providers or network bottlenecks |
| **System Resources** | Server CPU, GPU compute/memory, RAM, network I/O, database & API response times | Automatically triggers node autoscaling before capacity exhaustion |

Our proactive observability monitors the following metrics around the clock:
* **End-to-End Latency Profiles**: Latency distribution across ASR, TTS, LLM, Knowledge Base retrieval, time-to-first-token (TTFT), and raw network transmission, evaluated across **p75, p90, p95, and p99** percentiles.
* **Error Rates & Call Failures**: Real-time auditing of total failed call counts, transcription timeouts, LLM inference timeouts, and TTS synthesis error rates.
* **Hardware & Infrastructure Saturation**: Continuous telemetry across server CPU utilization, GPU memory and compute loads, system memory, network throughput, database queries, and API response latencies.

## What fallback and resilience features protect live calls?

Tavly AI incorporates automated resilience mechanisms designed to preserve call continuity under adverse network or provider conditions. These include **built-in LLM and TTS fallbacks and retries**, automated or manual failover voice routing, carrier allowlisting via **Branded Call/Verified Phone Numbers**, and comprehensive pre-production **testing features**.

* **Built-in LLM Fallback & Retries**: Automated retries and fallback mechanisms are built in directly to preserve conversational continuity when primary language models encounter timeouts or rate limits.
* **Built-in TTS Fallback & Retries**: Automated TTS fallbacks and retries are built in natively and can also be configured manually to swap between backup voice engines seamlessly. For implementation steps, see [TTS Fallback](/build/speech/tts-fallback).
* **Carrier Allowlisting & Call Efficiency**: Utilize [Branded Call / Verified Phone Number Features](/reliability/call-performance) to increase call pickup rates and ensure outbound calls are allowlisted by telecom carriers rather than flagged as spam.
* **Pre-Production Testing**: Validate agent behavior, stress-test conversational turns, and verify voice acoustics before production release using our suite of [Testing Features](/test/test-overview).
