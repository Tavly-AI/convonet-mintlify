> Tavly is a platform to build, test, deploy, and monitor AI voice and chat agents with telephony, prompts, tools, and analytics built in.

## Build

Tavly agents are node-based flows or single prompts, with call handling, a knowledge base, and integrations built in. See the full [build overview](/build/build-a-tavly-ai-agent-overview) for everything you can configure, starting with your agent type.
