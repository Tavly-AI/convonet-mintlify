## What is Tavly international calling?

**Tavly international calling** allows conversational AI voice agents to place high-quality outbound telephone calls to global destinations directly from your workspace. By leveraging underlying tier-1 carrier networks such as Twilio and Telnyx, businesses reach customers across North America, Europe, Asia, and Oceania without contracting local telecommunications providers in each country.

Global outbound calling integrates directly into standard enterprise telephony workflows:

* **Unified Workspace Management**: Initiate global calls from a centralized dashboard or single API without managing multi-vendor carrier relationships.
* **Global Customer Outreach**: For example, a US-based customer success team can deploy Tavly agents to contact users across India, the United Kingdom, and Australia using the same primary application workflows.
* **International Number Ingestion**: Purchase local international numbers directly or import foreign numbers from existing telecom carriers.

To purchase dedicated international lines, explore the [purchase a number](/deploy/purchase-number) guide.

## How do you place an international call with Tavly?

To **place an international call with Tavly**, initiate an outbound call using the Create Phone Call API or dashboard, specifying your destination in canonical international E.164 format with leading plus sign and country code. The recipient country must be included on the supported carrier destinations list.

Follow these standard practices when initiating cross-border telephone calls:

1. **Verify Country Support**: Ensure your destination appears in the carrier pricing tables above.
2. **Format in Canonical E.164**: Pass phone numbers formatted according to ITU-T standards (`+{country_code}{subscriber_number}`):
   * United Kingdom: `+442071838750`
   * India: `+919876543210`
   * Australia: `+61298765432`
3. **Execute API Request**: Dispatch the call using the standard [outbound calls](/deploy/outbound-calls) workflow:

```json theme={"dark"}
{
  "from_number": "+14157774444",
  "to_number": "+442071838750"
}
```

<Note>
  If using an **imported phone number** (via custom telephony or BYOC), international dialing permissions are governed entirely by your third-party telephony provider's geographic permissions.
</Note>

## How do you purchase international phone numbers?

To **purchase international phone numbers**, navigate to the Phone Numbers section in your Tavly console and search available inventory across supported foreign countries. Alternatively, teams can port or import existing numbers from external telephony providers using elastic SIP trunking or direct SIP URI interconnects.

Teams expanding global presence can acquire local caller IDs through two primary paths:

* **Direct Tavly Number Purchase**: Browse numbers in Canada, the United Kingdom, and international regions directly from the console inventory. Review the [purchase a number](/deploy/purchase-number) guide for regulatory verification requirements.
* **Custom Telephony Interconnects (BYOC)**: If you already own local DIDs in specific countries, connect them via [custom telephony](/deploy/custom-telephony) using providers such as Twilio, Telnyx, Vonage, or enterprise SBCs.
