## What is Tavly SMS capability and how does it work?

**Tavly SMS capability** equips conversational AI agents with bidirectional cellular text messaging. Voice agents can dispatch confirmations during live phone calls, receive caller photos or documents via MMS mid-conversation, and conduct autonomous two-way text conversations through dedicated chat agents using Twilio-backed numbers or compliant A2P 10DLC registration.

Tavly supports multiple conversational text architectures:

* **In-Call SMS Dispatch**: Voice agents text booking links, address confirmations, or receipts during active calls.
* **Mid-Call MMS Ingestion**: Callers text screenshots, IDs, or damage photos during a voice call; Tavly parses the image so the agent can respond contextually.
* **Two-Way Text Chat**: Autonomous text messaging powered by conversational [chat agents](/build/create-chat-agent).

<Warning>
  SMS is available for Tavly Twilio numbers and custom telephony numbers that have passed carrier A2P 10DLC review; Telnyx SMS is not supported currently. SMS is strictly limited to United States local phone numbers, excluding toll-free lines. The SMS add-on is automatically disabled for non-US numbers in the console.
</Warning>

## How do you enable SMS on Tavly Twilio numbers via A2P 10DLC?

To **enable SMS on Tavly Twilio numbers via A2P 10DLC**, complete three regulatory verification phases in your console: register an approved business profile, select a Low-Volume or Standard brand type, and submit an SMS campaign application with explicit sample messages, use case descriptions, and end-user opt-in workflows.

Enabling outbound SMS and two-way conversations on a Tavly Twilio number requires compliance approval from US cellular carriers:

### Application Overview and Fee Schedule

| Phase | Application Step | Fee Structure | Details |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Step 1** | Business Profile Registration | Free | Verified legal business identity and EIN |
| **Step 2** | Brand Registration | $4 (Low-Volume) or $45 (Standard) | One-time carrier fee based on message throughput |
| **Step 3** | Campaign Registration | $15 one-time fee | Defines use case, sample texts, and consent mechanism |
| **Ongoing** | Number SMS Add-On | $20/month per number + $0.01/SMS | Monthly maintenance and segment transmission fee |

### Sequential Registration Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get approved for a business profile">
    Create a new business profile or select an existing approved record. Follow the guidelines in the [Business Profile documentation](/build/telephony/business-profile) to ensure tax identification numbers and business addresses match government filings.

  <Step title="Select a brand type and get approved">

    Choose your operational brand tier:

      * One-time application fee: **$4**
      * Daily throughput limit: Up to **6,000 message segments/day** to US carriers (2,000/day to T-Mobile)
    * **Standard Brand**:
      * One-time application fee: **$45**
      * Daily throughput limit: Between **6,000 and 400,000 message segments/day** (2,000–200,000 to T-Mobile)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Submit the SMS campaign application">
    <Frame caption="The campaign application form: use case, description, sample messages, end-user consent, and privacy/terms URLs.">

    * **Use Case Description**: Clearly state why your organization sends SMS.
    * **Sample Messages**: Provide two realistic message templates illustrating opt-out clauses (e.g. *"Reply STOP to unsubscribe"*).
    * **End-User Consent**: Detail how users opt in (e.g. online forms, verbal agreement during calls).
    * **Privacy Policy and Terms URLs**: Public web links explicitly confirming customer numbers will not be shared or sold to third parties.

    Refer to [SMS campaign application guidelines](/deploy/sms-campaign-application) to avoid rejections.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How do you integrate your own Twilio number for SMS?

To **integrate your own Twilio number for SMS**, navigate to phone number settings, open Setup SMS Function, and supply your Twilio Account SID and Auth Token. After credentials verify, configure your Twilio Messaging Service inbound webhook to point toward Tavly to process incoming text and multimedia messages.

If you maintain existing SMS-enabled Twilio numbers, integrate them directly into your Tavly workspace:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Setup SMS Function">
    In the Tavly dashboard, select your phone number and click **Setup SMS Function** under the **Advanced Add-Ons** section.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Provide your Twilio API credentials">
    Input your credentials located in the Twilio Console under Account Info:

    * **Account SID**: Your unique alphanumeric Twilio account identifier.
    * **Twilio Auth Token**: Your primary secret authorization token.

    <Frame caption="The Setup SMS Function dialog asking for the Twilio Account SID and Auth Token.">
      <img src="https://mintcdn.com/retellai/_y-iu8M6jQ67ZrqV/images/custom-twilio.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=_y-iu8M6jQ67ZrqV&q=85&s=4963b87c5017b0a13c2e7748e4ccdb3c" alt="Setup SMS Function dialog with SMS Provider set to Twilio and empty fields for Account SID and Twilio Auth Token" style={{ maxHeight: 560 }} width="1062" height="1046" data-path="images/custom-twilio.png" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Update Twilio Messaging Service routing">
    After verification, update your Twilio Messaging Service configuration so incoming messages are routed via the phone number's webhook setting (`useInboundWebhookOnNumber`), allowing Tavly to ingest incoming texts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Ensure your external Twilio number has passed A2P 10DLC campaign registration within your own Twilio account prior to connecting. Consult [Twilio A2P 10DLC documentation](https://www.twilio.com/docs/proxy/flex-a2p-10dlc).
</Note>

## How do you use a pre-approved Tavly SMS pool without A2P?

To **use a pre-approved Tavly SMS pool without A2P**, select the Tavly shared number pool option in your agent SMS node or tool configuration. This allows voice agents to immediately dispatch standardized in-call notification templates without carrier registration fees, though custom text prompts and inbound two-way conversations remain unsupported.

If your team only needs to text callers links or receipts during an active phone conversation, bypass the weeks-long A2P approval process entirely using Tavly's pre-approved carrier number pool:

* **Instant Availability**: No registration forms, setup delays, or carrier application fees.
* **Automated Selection**: A compliant number is selected dynamically for each call; all texts sent during that call originate from that same number.
* **Standardized Templates**: Content is restricted to preset transactional templates to adhere to cellular carrier anti-spam regulations.

<Warning>
  Shared pool numbers do not support custom freeform text prompts, custom variables, or inbound two-way SMS conversations.
</Warning>

**In-call SMS and mid-call MMS reception** allow voice agents to dispatch text confirmations or ingest caller multimedia during active conversations. When callers text photos, screenshots, or receipts to your Twilio number, Tavly processes the attachment in real time and injects visual context directly into ongoing voice dialogues.

### In-Call Outbound SMS

Configure your agent to trigger text messages during conversations:

* **Conversation Flow Agents**: Add and configure the [SMS Node](/build/conversation-flow/sms-node).
* **Single / Multi Prompt Agents**: Attach the [Send SMS Tool](/build/single-multi-prompt/send-sms).

## How do you configure two-way SMS conversations with chat agents?

To **configure two-way SMS conversations with chat agents**, open your phone number settings and bind separate AI chat agents to the Inbound SMS Agent and Outbound SMS Agent selectors. Incoming texts trigger the chat agent immediately, while outbound SMS conversations launch programmatically via API or dashboard.

Once your phone number has an active A2P SMS add-on or connected Twilio credentials, attach [chat agents](/build/create-chat-agent) to automate two-way text messaging:

<Frame caption="The Inbound SMS agent and Outbound SMS agent selectors on the phone number page.">

### Routing Controls

* **Inbound Handling**: Assign a chat agent to **Inbound SMS Agent** to respond automatically to incoming texts.
* **Outbound Initiation**: Dispatch proactive outbound SMS via the dashboard or through the [Create Outbound SMS API](/api-references/create-sms-chat).
* **Dynamic Interception**: Use the [Inbound Call and SMS Webhook](/deploy/inbound-call-webhook) to filter numbers, inject customer context, or decline messages dynamically.

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to complete an A2P application to receive SMS during phone calls?">
    No. Receiving SMS and MMS during active voice calls works out of the box for Tavly Twilio numbers without A2P application. An approved A2P 10DLC campaign is only required to send SMS from your own number or conduct two-way text chat.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is Telnyx supported for SMS messaging?">
    Not currently. SMS capabilities are supported exclusively on Tavly Twilio numbers and custom Twilio telephony integrations that have completed A2P 10DLC registration.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I send SMS from a toll-free telephone number?">
    No. Under US cellular carrier standards, A2P 10DLC is restricted to 10-digit local phone numbers and excludes toll-free lines. The SMS add-on is disabled for toll-free numbers.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I send custom message prompts without going through A2P?">
    No. If you skip A2P registration and send from Tavly's shared pool of approved numbers, message content is restricted to preset, unmodifiable transactional templates.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long does the A2P review process take?">
    Carrier manual review typically takes 2 to 3 weeks. You will receive an automated email when your application is approved or if revisions are requested.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
