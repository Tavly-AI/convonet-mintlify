## What is a telephony business profile and why is it mandatory?

A **telephony business profile** is an official organizational identity record registered with telecommunications carrier databases. It is a mandatory regulatory prerequisite for deploying Tavly AI verified phone numbers, branded caller ID, and A2P 10DLC messaging campaigns, ensuring your outbound calling traffic receives legitimate carrier trust and high pickup rates.

Under federal telecommunication guidelines and carrier reputation frameworks (such as STIR/SHAKEN Level-A attestation), voice providers cannot issue authenticated caller IDs or register custom branding without verified corporate identity. Establishing your business profile links your legal entity to your telephony assets, protecting your numbers against erroneous [Spam Likely flags](/deploy/calls/spam-likely-overview).

### Telephony Capabilities Enabled by a Verified Profile

* **[Verified Phone Numbers](/deploy/calls/verified-phone)**: Cryptographically attest phone lines to eliminate negative spam flags and carrier call blocking.
* **[Branded Calls (Rich Call Data)](/deploy/calls/branded-calls)**: Display your official company name, verified badge, and call reason on recipient mobile screens.
* **[A2P 10DLC SMS Messaging](/deploy/enable-sms)**: Provide the baseline brand approval needed to run compliant [SMS campaigns](/deploy/sms-campaign-application).

## How do you complete the business profile form and documentation requirements?

To **complete the business profile form**, provide your exact legal business entity name, government registration tax identifier (EIN), physical corporate address, authorized physical contact number, and public company website. Carrier compliance algorithms verify these entries against official state registries, rejecting virtual VoIP numbers and mismatched entity names.

### Mandatory Carrier Validation Standards

To ensure first-pass approval from telecommunication vetting agencies, review each field against these strict carrier standards:

| Information Requirement | Validation Rule & Compliance Standard | Common Rejection Pitfall |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Legal Business Name** | Must match state incorporation or IRS CP 575 confirmation exactly. | Using an informal brand name or abbreviation without filing a DBA. |
| **Business Registration (EIN / Tax ID)** | Must match the official 9-digit federal Employer Identification Number. | Submitting an incorrect state tax number or personal SSN. |
| **Physical Business Address** | Must be a verifiable commercial office or registered headquarters. | PO Boxes, virtual offices, or mailbox rental services are prohibited. |
| **Contact Phone Number** | Must be a direct physical line or verified mobile phone line. | Virtual VoIP, toll-free, or unassigned telephone numbers are rejected. |
| **Public Corporate Website** | Must be live, functional, and display your legal business identity. | Inaccessible staging links, placeholder domains, or missing privacy pages. |

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How long does it take for a business profile to be verified?">
    Initial automated screening takes between 1 to 2 business days. If manual carrier documentation review is triggered, verification can require 3 to 5 business days.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I edit my business profile details after submission?">
    If the profile is in draft or rejected status, you can freely edit fields and resubmit. If the profile is already approved, modifying foundational legal details requires submitting an update request or registering an updated profile.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does creating a business profile incur any billing charges?">
    No. Registering and maintaining a business profile in the Tavly dashboard is completely free. Fees apply only when registering specific downstream carrier add-ons, such as A2P 10DLC campaign reviews.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to my phone numbers if my profile is rejected?">
    Your phone numbers will continue to function for standard inbound and outbound voice calls. However, premium features such as carrier verified status, branded display, and custom A2P outbound SMS will remain disabled until discrepancies are resolved.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
