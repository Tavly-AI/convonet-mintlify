## What is the Five9 and Tavly pre-built SIP trunk integration?

The **Five9 and Tavly pre-built SIP trunk integration** connects Five9 Virtual Contact Center (VCC) directly to Tavly conversational voice agents over a dedicated carrier-grade SIP interconnect. Because the trunk infrastructure is pre-established between both platforms, teams avoid building custom trunks and simply route numbers via Five9 IVR scripts.

Once this telecommunications connection is established, your contact center can:

* **Transfer inbound calls**: Route live calls seamlessly from a Five9 IVR script to an active Tavly conversational agent.
* **Dispatch outbound calls**: Initiate automated outbound voice interactions from Tavly displaying your verified Five9 number as the caller ID.

**Enterprise Use Case Example:** A healthcare system utilizes Five9 IVR as the initial customer gateway. During core business hours, the IVR routes patients to live contact center agents; outside business hours, the same script routes callers to a Tavly conversational agent that securely fulfills prescription refills and schedules provider callbacks.

Configuration takes approximately 20 minutes, plus Five9 Support turnaround time to complete the carrier trunk routing request. For baseline concepts on how Tavly interfaces with external carriers, see the [custom telephony overview](/deploy/custom-telephony).

## What prerequisites and coordination are required before setup?

The **prerequisites and coordination** required before setup include provisioning a pseudo number within your Five9 Numbers Inventory, submitting a trunk routing ticket to Five9 Support, configuring a Tavly voice agent, and scheduling joint deployment sessions between your organization, your Five9 representative, and the Tavly engineering support team.

Review the foundational readiness requirements below before initiating deployment:

### Deployment Prerequisites

| Requirement | Details | Verification Criteria |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tavly Account** | Configured with an active [conversational voice agent](/build/overview). | Agent prompt and voice models tested in playground. |
| **Five9 VCC Administrator** | Desktop application (v13.x) with administrator access. | Permissions to create IVR scripts and inbound campaigns. |
| **Phone Number** | Number provisioned in your Five9 Numbers Inventory. | Must be the dedicated internal **pseudo number**. |
| **Trunk Routing** | Five9 Support routing ticket activated. | Confirms traffic routes to Tavly over the pre-built trunk. |

<Warning>
  Trunk routing is provisioned exclusively by Five9 Support. Submit a support ticket with your Five9 account team prior to modifying your IVR scripts. If trunk routing is inactive, call transfers will drop immediately upon execution.
</Warning>

### Responsibility Assignment Matrix

Enterprise deployments require collaborative execution across administrative teams:

| Stage | Action Item | Execution Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | Import Five9 number into Tavly | Customer administrator via the Tavly console. |
| **2–5** | Configure IVR script, transfer node, and campaign | **Customer and Five9 representative** in Five9 VCC Administrator. |
| **6** | End-to-end integration and media testing | **Customer, Five9 representative, and Tavly support engineers jointly.** |

<Note>
  Schedule dedicated working sessions with your Five9 representative for IVR and campaign configuration, and align with Tavly support for joint validation testing. Coordinating these teams in advance prevents project stalls.
</Note>

## How do you import a Five9 pseudo number into Tavly?

To **import a Five9 pseudo number into Tavly**, open the Tavly console, input your Five9-assigned pseudo number in E.164 format, specify your regional Five9 SBC termination IP with TLS port 5061, set outbound transport to TLS, and leave username and password fields blank since digest authentication is disabled.

Follow these step-by-step instructions to register your number:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your Five9 number in Tavly">
    1. In the Tavly dashboard, open **Phone Numbers** and select **Connect to your own number**.
    2. Enter your phone number in standard E.164 format (e.g. `+19995550106`).
    3. In the **Termination URI** field, enter the Five9 SBC signaling IP matching your data center, followed by TLS port `5061` (for example, `208.69.29.18:5061`).
    4. Set **Outbound Transport** to `TLS`.
    5. Leave **SIP Trunk User Name** and **SIP Trunk Password** blank. The pre-built trunk does not utilize digest authentication; entering credentials will cause call handshakes to fail.
    6. Click **Save**, and assign your Tavly conversational agent to this number for inbound and outbound routing.

    <Frame caption="SIP trunk settings for an imported Five9 number in the Tavly dashboard.">
      <img src="https://mintcdn.com/retellai/u27PZbC05UlJ3sJ0/images/deploy/five9/five9-retell-import-number.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=u27PZbC05UlJ3sJ0&q=85&s=e6404ab9f17ff4b2a47bcc2e50c19383" alt="Edit SIP Trunk Phone Number Settings modal with the Termination URI set to 208.69.29.18:5061 (1), Outbound Transport set to TLS (2), and the Save button (3). SIP Trunk User Name and Password are left empty." style={{ maxHeight: 560 }} width="616" height="661" data-path="images/deploy/five9/five9-retell-import-number.png" />
    </Frame>

    <Warning>
      Import the **pseudo number** provisioned by Five9, not the customer-facing external DID. Importing the external DID is the most frequent cause of transfer disconnects.
    </Warning>

    <Note>
      No IP whitelisting is required on external firewalls. The pre-built interconnect between Tavly and Five9 is pre-authorized at both carrier edges.
    </Note>

    You can also import numbers programmatically via the [Import Number API](/api-references/import-phone-number).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Identify your regional Five9 SBC signaling IP">
    Select the termination URI that corresponds to the specific Five9 data center hosting your VCC domain:

    | Five9 Data Center | Geographic Region | Termination URI |
    | :--- | :--- | :--- |
    | **Atlanta (ATL06)** | US East | `208.69.29.18:5061` |
    | **Santa Clara (SCL06)** | US West | `162.213.153.61:5061` |
    | **Montréal (MTL10)** | Canada | `74.114.192.15:5061` |
    | **Montréal (MTL3)** | Canada | `74.114.193.15:5061` |
    | **London (LND03)** | United Kingdom | `212.187.211.56:5061` |
    | **Frankfurt (FRK)** | Germany | `185.111.41.18:5061` |
    | **Amsterdam (AMS03)** | Netherlands | `185.111.42.17:5061` |
    | **Tokyo (TOK)** | Japan | `103.169.228.18:5061` |
    | **Sydney (SYD)** | Australia | `103.169.229.18:5061` |
    | **São Paulo (SAO)** | Brazil | `209.14.129.18:5061` |

    <Note>
      Always verify the termination IP with Five9 Support before going live. Five9 provisions trunk routing per data center, and their support team will confirm your exact target IP.
    </Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How do you configure the Five9 IVR script and transfer nodes?

To configure the **Five9 IVR script and transfer nodes**, open Five9 VCC Administrator, create an IVR script, add a ThirdPartyTransfer module, set the third-party number to Constant with your imported E.164 pseudo number, leave return options unchecked for single-step blind transfer, and save your call flow.

Execute these steps inside Five9 VCC Administrator with your Five9 representative:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the IVR script">
    1. Open the Five9 **VCC Administrator** desktop application.
    2. In the left-hand navigation tree, select **IVR Scripts**.
    3. Click the **+** button in the top toolbar to create a new script.
    4. Provide a distinct identifier (e.g. `TavlyAITransfer`).

    <Frame caption="The IVR Scripts list in Five9 VCC Administrator.">
      <img src="https://mintcdn.com/retellai/u27PZbC05UlJ3sJ0/images/deploy/five9/five9-ivr-scripts-list.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=u27PZbC05UlJ3sJ0&q=85&s=a957d584f1a1bc55a0cbecf24087a897" alt="Five9 VCC Administrator window with IVR Scripts selected in the left-hand tree (1) and the toolbar's add button ringed (2). The list shows a script named RetellAITransfer alongside Sample_IVR." style={{ maxHeight: 560 }} width="1038" height="496" data-path="images/deploy/five9/five9-ivr-scripts-list.png" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Insert and configure the ThirdPartyTransfer module">
    1. In your call flow diagram, add a **ThirdPartyTransfer** node at the desired handoff stage.

    <Frame caption="A ThirdPartyTransfer node placed at the end of an IVR call flow.">
      <img src="https://mintcdn.com/retellai/u27PZbC05UlJ3sJ0/images/deploy/five9/five9-ivr-thirdpartytransfer-node.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=u27PZbC05UlJ3sJ0&q=85&s=337b0314c267ac701cb11736e595e7e3" alt="Five9 IVR script editor showing a call flow of IncomingCall4 to Play4 to a ThirdPartyTransfer node, with the ThirdPartyTransfer node ringed (1)." style={{ maxHeight: 560 }} width="642" height="390" data-path="images/deploy/five9/five9-ivr-thirdpartytransfer-node.png" />
    </Frame>

    2. Double-click the **ThirdPartyTransfer** node to open its property settings.
    3. Set **Third Party Number** to **Constant**.
    4. Enter the pseudo number imported into Tavly in exact E.164 format (e.g. `+19995550106`).
    5. Leave **Return After 3rd Party Call** unchecked (enables single-step blind transfer so Five9 releases the leg).
    6. Click **Save**, then save the complete IVR script.

## How do you create inbound campaigns and schedule IVR rules?

To create **inbound campaigns and schedule IVR rules**, provision an Inbound Campaign in Five9 VCC Administrator, navigate to the IVR tab, create a schedule rule linking your transfer script, set operational hours and days, enable the Voice channel, and bind your customer-facing DID to the campaign.

Configure your campaign properties following these steps:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the Inbound Campaign">
    1. In Five9 VCC Administrator, select **Campaigns** from the left navigation tree.
    2. Click the **+** button in the toolbar to create a new campaign.
    3. Set campaign type to **Inbound** and assign a name (e.g. `TavlyAIAgentCampaign`).

    <Frame caption="The Campaigns list in Five9 VCC Administrator.">
      <img src="https://mintcdn.com/retellai/u27PZbC05UlJ3sJ0/images/deploy/five9/five9-campaigns-list.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=u27PZbC05UlJ3sJ0&q=85&s=e16562f44cc436b03894723c5c81956d" alt="Five9 VCC Administrator window with Campaigns selected in the left-hand tree (1) and the toolbar's add button ringed (2). The list shows RetellAIAgentCampaign with type Inbound." style={{ maxHeight: 560 }} width="1038" height="478" data-path="images/deploy/five9/five9-campaigns-list.png" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Attach the IVR schedule rule">
    1. Double-click the campaign to open **Properties**.
    2. Go to the **IVR** tab and click **Add** to create a schedule rule.
    3. Name the rule, open the **IVR Script** dropdown, and select your transfer script (`TavlyAITransfer`).
    4. Select your operational schedule (**All day long** or specific hours/days).
    5. Under **Five9 VCC Channels**, ensure **Voice** is checked.
    6. Click **OK**, and save the campaign.

    <Frame caption="The campaign's IVR schedule rule pointing to the Tavly transfer script.">
      <img src="https://mintcdn.com/retellai/u27PZbC05UlJ3sJ0/images/deploy/five9/five9-campaign-ivr-schedule.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=u27PZbC05UlJ3sJ0&q=85&s=111f7ed2b301dd0fab046e1019ef13df" alt="Schedule rule dialog inside campaign properties: the IVR Script dropdown open (1) with RetellAITransfer selected (2), schedule options set to All day long (3), the Voice channel ticked (4), and the OK button (5)." style={{ maxHeight: 560 }} width="1125" height="783" data-path="images/deploy/five9/five9-campaign-ivr-schedule.png" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Execute Joint Testing">
    Conduct validation testing jointly with your Five9 representative and Tavly support engineers:

    * **Inbound Call Test**: Dial the campaign DID from an external carrier. Confirm the call triggers the IVR, executes the transfer, and is answered cleanly by the Tavly voice agent.
    * **Outbound Call Test**: Place an outbound call from Tavly via the console or API and confirm the callee sees the expected caller ID.
    * **CDR Reconciliation**: Compare Tavly call logs against Five9 call detail reports to confirm call duration and disconnect causes match.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How do you troubleshoot Five9 SIP trunking and transfer issues?

To troubleshoot **Five9 SIP trunking and transfer issues**, verify that your imported number matches the Five9 pseudo number rather than customer DIDs, confirm the termination URI specifies port 5061 over TLS, check that Five9 Support activated trunk routing, and ensure RFC 2833 DTMF payload 101 is negotiated.

Refer to the diagnostic reference matrix below to isolate and resolve operational faults:

### Troubleshooting Matrix

| Symptom | Root Cause | Resolution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Call reaches IVR but drops at transfer** | Pseudo number missing from Five9 Numbers Inventory or trunk routing inactive | Raise a ticket with Five9 Support to verify number inventory assignment and trunk routing. |
| **Calls do not connect at all** | Incorrect Termination URI or cleartext transport | Ensure the Termination URI specifies the correct data center SBC IP with port `:5061` and **Outbound Transport** set to `TLS`. |
| **Transfer connects but agent never answers** | Customer-facing external DID was imported instead of pseudo number | Re-import the number in Tavly using the Five9-provisioned **pseudo number**. |
| **DTMF tones not detected** | Non-standard DTMF payload negotiated | Ensure DTMF is set to RFC 2833 with payload type `101`. Inspect signaling via [PCAP analysis](/reliability/debug-calls-pcap). |
| **Transfer fails with invalid number error** | Number format mismatch | Ensure both Tavly and the Five9 ThirdPartyTransfer node use canonical E.164 format with leading `+`. |
| **IVR script never triggers** | Inactive campaign rule or missing channel | Verify the campaign schedule is active and confirm the **Voice** channel is ticked on the IVR rule. |

For Tavly platform diagnostics and disconnect codes, consult [Debug outbound connection issues](/reliability/debug-outbound-call).

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to build a custom SIP trunk between Five9 and Tavly?">
    No. A dedicated pre-built SIP trunk interconnect already exists between Five9 and Tavly. You only need Five9 Support to route your provisioned number over that trunk, and then import that number into your Tavly console.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I import the DID or the pseudo number?">
    Always import the **pseudo number** provisioned by Five9. Do not use the customer-facing DID. Importing the DID is the most common cause of transfer failures.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to whitelist any IP addresses?">
    No. The pre-built trunk between Tavly and Five9 is pre-authorized at both carrier edges, so no external firewall modifications or IP allowlisting are necessary.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why should SIP Trunk User Name and Password stay blank?">
    The pre-built trunk does not utilize digest authentication. Entering credentials causes Tavly to attempt an authenticated SIP registration that the trunk rejects, causing calls to fail.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can the Tavly agent transfer the call back to a human Five9 agent?">
    Yes. Add a transfer node to your Tavly agent that hands the call to an inbound Five9 campaign number or SIP URI. See [call transfer node](/build/setup/conversation-flow/add-nodes/call-transfer-node) for conversation flow agents, or [transfer call](/build/setup/single-multi-prompt/transfer-call) for single/multi-prompt agents. Use [custom SIP headers](/build/telephony/sip-headers) to propagate customer and session metadata.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How many simultaneous calls can I run through this trunk?">
    Concurrency limits depend on your Tavly tier [concurrency capacity](/deploy/concurrency) alongside Five9's trunk-level channel provisioning. Verify both capacity limits with your account teams prior to launching high-volume campaigns.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
