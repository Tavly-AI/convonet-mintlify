Purchase a phone number from Tavly directly in the dashboard or through the API. Tavly manages these numbers, so you don't have to set up any telephony infrastructure.

<Note>
  Currently we only support purchase of US and Canada numbers and support making calls to [15 countries](/deploy/international-call). If you are looking to use numbers from other countries, or to make calls to more countries, or to use your own telephony provider, check out [Custom Telephony guide](/deploy/custom-telephony).
</Note>

## What are Tavly-managed phone numbers?

**Tavly-managed phone numbers** provide turnkey telephony connectivity for voice agents without requiring third-party carrier accounts. Teams can provision United States and Canadian local or toll-free numbers directly in the dashboard or via API, assigning inbound and outbound agents instantly without managing SIP trunking, telecom contracts, or telephony infrastructure.

Tavly provides turnkey carrier provisioning across multiple tier-one telecom providers:

| Number Type / Provider | Supported Region | Monthly Rental Cost | Inbound Usage Rate | Outbound Calling Support |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Twilio Local** | United States (`+1`) | **$2.00 / month** | Standard voice rate | Standard voice rate |
| **Twilio Toll-Free** | United States (`+1-800/888...`) | **$5.00 / month** | **$0.06 / min** + voice rate | Standard voice rate |
| **Twilio Canada** | Canada (`+1`) | **$2.00 / month** | Standard voice rate | Standard voice rate |
| **Telnyx Local** | United States (`+1`) | **$2.00 / month** | Standard voice rate | Standard voice rate |

## How do you purchase a phone number from the dashboard?

To purchase a number from the dashboard, navigate to the **Phone Numbers** screen, click **Buy Number**, select your preferred area code, and bind an inbound or outbound agent. After checkout, assign a descriptive nickname for organization; the number immediately begins accepting inbound calls or making outbound dispatches.

You can purchase a number and bind agents to it from the dashboard. You can optionally specify the area codes you want to purchase from.

## How do you purchase and configure phone numbers via API?

To provision phone numbers programmatically, invoke the **Create Phone Number API** endpoint with your desired agent identifiers. The API allows developers to specify inbound and outbound agent bindings, configure webhook parameters, or leave inbound agents unassigned to prevent callbacks during outbound cold calling campaigns.

Check out the [Create Phone Number API Reference](/api-references/create-phone-number) for all the parameters you can use programmatically:

* **Indefinite ownership**: Phone numbers are yours once purchased, and can be used indefinitely. Find numbers you own via [List Phone Numbers](/api-references/list-phone-numbers).
* **Dual agent bindings**: You can assign different inbound and outbound agents to the number.
* **Callback suppression**: If you don't want a user to be able to call this number (for example, when conducting [outbound calls](/deploy/outbound-calls) where callbacks are unsupported), leave `inbound_agent_id` unset.

## How does phone number pricing and billing work?

**Phone number pricing** operates on a flat monthly lease model starting at **$2.00 per month** for standard US and Canadian numbers, and **$5.00 per month** for US toll-free numbers. Recurring number charges are billed at the close of each billing cycle until released, with toll-free inbound calls incurring an additional $0.06 per minute.

Tavly supports managed numbers through both **Twilio** and **Telnyx**:

* **Twilio**:
  * US local numbers: **$2/month**
  * US toll-free numbers: **$5/month**
  * Canadian numbers: **$2/month**
* **Telnyx**:
  * US numbers only: **$2/month**

<Note>
  Toll-free numbers cost $0.06 per minute for inbound calls. Outbound calls are charged at the same rate as regular U.S. numbers.
</Note>

The monthly number fee is billed to your payment method at the end of each billing cycle (prorated if purchased mid-month on credit-based accounts) and recurs until the number is released. See the [billing overview](/accounts/billing) for how number charges appear on your invoice.

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I use a phone number I already own?">
    Yes. Connect your own telephony provider through [custom telephony](/deploy/custom-telephony), or [import an existing number](/api-references/import-phone-number) into Tavly.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I buy a number outside the US and Canada?">
    Not directly. Tavly-managed numbers are US and Canada only. To use numbers from other countries, connect your own provider via [custom telephony](/deploy/custom-telephony). Tavly-managed numbers can still place calls to [15 countries](/deploy/international-call).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I release a number I no longer need?">
    Delete it from the phone number page in the dashboard, or call the [Delete Phone Number API](/api-references/delete-phone-number). The monthly fee stops once the number is released.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
