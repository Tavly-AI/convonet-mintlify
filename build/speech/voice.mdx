> Add custom voices to your Tavly agent — search ElevenLabs community voices, import a voice clone, or train a clone for a unique brand-specific sound.

If you find the existing voice doesn't meet your needs, you can add a custom voice. There's a limit of 100 custom voices per account. If you need more, please contact us via the <a href="https://support.tavlyai.com/">Customer Support Portal</a> or [support@tavlyai.com](mailto:support@tavlyai.com).

## Add Custom Voice

In the voice selector, you can click "Add custom voice" to search and add publicly available community voices.

### What to say in the recordings

Cloning captures how the voice sounds, not what it says, so you don't need to read a specific script. Any natural speech works. For the best result:

* Speak in the tone, pace, and energy you want the agent to use on calls. The clone reproduces the delivery it hears.
* Use continuous, conversational speech rather than isolated words or long pauses.
* Record a single speaker with no background noise, music, or echo.
* Avoid heavily edited or compressed audio. A clean microphone recording works better than audio captured from a phone or video call.

### File limits

File limits depend on the voice provider you clone with. Inworld voice cloning accepts up to 3 files and 16 MB of audio in total, while Cartesia, MiniMax, and platform clones take a single file. ElevenLabs clones accept up to 25 files via the [Clone Voice API](/api-references/clone-voice).
