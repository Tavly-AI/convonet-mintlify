## What are Custom Voices?

Custom voices allow you to deploy specific acoustic models when standard library voices do not match your brand identity. You can browse shared community libraries or train bespoke voice clones using your own audio.

Each account natively supports up to **100 custom voices**. To request a quota increase beyond this limit, submit a ticket through the <a href="https://support.tavlyai.com/">Customer Support Portal</a> or email [support@tavlyai.com](mailto:support@tavlyai.com).

## How do you browse Community Voices?

You can directly search, preview, and integrate public community voices (such as those from ElevenLabs) directly from the Tavly dashboard.

1. Navigate to your specific agent configuration panel.
2. Open the primary voice selection dropdown menu.
3. Click **Add custom voice** to browse the shared community library.

## What are the Audio Recording Standards?

Cloning algorithms train on acoustic timbre, cadence, and inflection—not semantic text. To maximize clone fidelity, you must record high-quality, uncompressed audio that perfectly matches your intended deployment tone.

* **Match Production Cadence:** Deliver speech with the specific tone, tempo, and emotional projection intended for live phone calls. The trained clone directly mirrors the source delivery.
* **Maintain Conversational Flow:** Speak in coherent, flowing sentences rather than fragmented word lists or punctuated pauses.
* **Eliminate Acoustic Interference:** Capture audio from an isolated speaker without ambient background sounds, room reverberation, or music.
* **Use Clean Raw Audio:** Prioritize uncompressed microphone recordings over compressed teleconference streams or cellular call audio.

## What are the Provider File Requirements?

Audio upload constraints vary strictly based on the underlying synthesis provider chosen to train the clone.

* **Inworld:** Up to 3 audio files with a maximum cumulative payload of 16 MB.
* **Cartesia, MiniMax, and Platform:** Exactly 1 audio file.
* **ElevenLabs:** Up to 25 individual files when provisioned programmatically using the [Clone Voice API](/api-references/clone-voice).
