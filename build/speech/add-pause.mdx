## Why control Speech Pacing?

While the global speaking rate is governed by your core agent settings, certain conversational moments require deliberate, localized slowing. This is essential when reciting sensitive alphanumeric strings like account codes, phone numbers, or mailing addresses.

## How do you insert a Short Pause?

You can explicitly instruct your language model to insert acoustic pauses by placing a hyphen (`-`) flanked by physical spaces directly between terms.

```json theme={"dark"}
The number is 2 - 1 - 3 - 4
```

<Warning> **Formatting Constraint:** You absolutely must include whitespace on both sides of each dash (` - `). If you omit the spaces, the synthesis engine parses the mark as a compound hyphen (like "x-ray") and will not pause.</Warning>

## How do you insert Extended Pauses?

To create significantly longer periods of silence between spoken items, prompt the model to output a continuous sequence of multiple dashes separated by spaces. 

```json theme={"dark"}
The number is 2 -  -  -  - 1 -  -  -  - 3
// Notice the double spaces between the dashes
```

Every additional spaced hyphen incrementally increases the exact duration of the silence before the voice synthesis resumes.
