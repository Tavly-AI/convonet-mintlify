> Tavly platform voices are a curated library tuned for phone conversations — clear at telecom bitrates, with automatic TTS fallback and no extra setup.

Platform voices are Tavly's curated voice library, fine-tuned specifically for conversational AI over the phone. They handle fillers, pacing, and conversational rhythm well, and are calibrated for clarity at telecom bitrates. For how they compare with third-party providers like MiniMax, Cartesia, and Inworld, see the [TTS provider comparison](/build/speech/tts-provider-comparison).

When using a platform voice, fallback is handled automatically to maintain the same voice experience — you do not need to configure a TTS fallback plan in Security & Fallback Settings.

<Note>
  If you are using a non-platform voice, you will need to [configure TTS fallback](/build/speech/tts-fallback) manually.
</Note>

## Select a platform voice

Platform voices are available in the voice selector in your agent settings. You can preview each voice directly in the dashboard before selecting.

## Custom voice cloning

You can clone your own voice as a platform voice by setting `voice_provider` to `platform` when calling the Clone Voice {/* broken-link: /api-references/clone-voice target page does not exist in this repo */} API. The cloned voice will behave the same as a built-in platform voice — fallback is handled automatically and you do not need to configure TTS fallback separately.
