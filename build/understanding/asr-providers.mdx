## What is Speech Recognition (ASR)?

**Speech Recognition**, or ASR, is the critical infrastructure layer that transcribes your caller's spoken audio into raw text for the LLM to process. 

Tavly automatically evaluates and selects the optimal ASR provider dynamically based on the exact languages your agent is configured to handle. You do not need to manually configure this routing layer.

<Note> **Performance Metrics:** The latency figures and routing logic discussed below are based on strict internal testing. Actual runtime results may vary slightly depending on audio degradation, caller accents, and network conditions.</Note>

## Which ASR providers does Tavly use?

Tavly relies on three industry-leading speech recognition engines: Azure, Deepgram, and Soniox. Each provider is aggressively optimized for a different operational profile.

### Azure
* **Best for:** Extremely wide coverage of individual languages, particularly excelling at rare or localized dialects.
* **Language capability:** Supports a massive range of languages, but operates strictly in single-language mode (no dynamic code-switching).
* **Average Latency:** ~530ms.

### Deepgram
* **Best for:** Aggressive low-latency transcription for highly common languages.
* **Language capability:** Can dynamically code-switch across 10 core languages (English, Spanish, French, German, Hindi, Russian, Portuguese, Japanese, Italian, and Dutch).
* **Average Latency:** ~300ms.

### Soniox
* **Best for:** Complex multilingual agents that require callers to seamlessly swap between diverse languages mid-sentence.
* **Language capability:** Operates a single unified model covering 60+ languages with identical support across both single and multi-language modes.
* **Average Latency:** ~490ms.

## How does Tavly route between providers?

Tavly dynamically calculates the ideal ASR provider for every call based on your agent's explicitly configured optimization goals and required languages.

* **Optimized for Latency:** Tavly aggressively prioritizes the fastest provider (usually Deepgram) that safely supports your exact language stack.
* **Optimized for Accuracy:** Tavly shifts priority to the provider with the highest historical transcription accuracy for your specific linguistic requirements.
* **Multilingual constraints:** If you configure a [Multilingual Agent](/agent/multilingual), the exact matrix of selected languages instantly filters out any ASR providers incapable of processing them simultaneously.

If you select a combination of languages that no single ASR provider on Earth currently supports together, the dashboard simply will not allow you to save the configuration.

For a detailed matrix of which provider supports which exact language codes, review the [Language Support by Provider](/build/language-support) guide.
