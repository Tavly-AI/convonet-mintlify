## Why do payments fail and how does it affect your Tavly agents?

Payments fail primarily due to **issuing bank fraud filters**, automated **international transaction blocks**, expired card credentials, or **insufficient credit balances**. A failed transaction prevents prepaid credit recharges and invoice settlements, causing Tavly to **block new incoming and outgoing calls** until account balances are restored.

When a payment fails on your default payment method, Tavly cannot execute automated auto-recharge top-ups or settle end-of-month subscription invoices for active phone numbers, knowledge bases, and concurrency tiers.

<Warning>
  **Service Interruption Risk**: For credit-based workspaces, calls are halted immediately when the prepaid balance hits **\$0.00**. For legacy accounts, overdue invoices risk account suspension. Promptly resolve failed charges to maintain production telephony availability.
</Warning>

### Common Payment Failure Root Causes

| Failure Symptom | Most Common Root Cause | Recommended Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Card Declined: Generic** | Issuing bank automated fraud prevention or cross-border payment restriction. | Contact your bank to explicitly allowlist transactions from Tavly. |
| **Auto-Recharge Failed** | Card expired, credit limit reached, or temporary bank outage during the recharge trigger. | Update card details in the Stripe portal or add a backup card. |
| **3D Secure / OTP Challenge** | Bank mandates interactive authentication that unattended background recharges cannot complete. | Request that your bank exempt recurring charges from Tavly from step-up authentication. |
| **Subscription Invoice Failed** | End-of-cycle card charge declined for phone numbers, CPS, or concurrency tiers. | Settle the invoice manually via the Billing tab after updating payment info. |

## How do you resolve a failed payment step-by-step?

To resolve a failed payment, **contact your card-issuing bank** to authorize charges from Tavly, retry the transaction in the **Billing tab**, and provide **written bank authorization** to support if blocks persist. Alternatively, add a secondary credit or debit card via the **Stripe Customer Portal**.

Execute the following troubleshooting steps in sequence:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check with Your Bank and Request Allowlisting">
    1. **Contact Your Card Issuer**: Call the phone number on the back of your card and speak with the fraud or authorizations department.
    2. **Verify Blocked Transactions**: Ask the representative to locate the blocked charge attempts from Tavly (processed via Stripe).
    3. **Request Merchant Allowlisting**: Instruct the bank to permanently allowlist and pre-authorize future transactions from Tavly.
    4. **Retry the Payment**: Return to the **Billing** tab in your Tavly dashboard and retry purchasing credits or paying the open invoice.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Submit Bank Written Confirmation if Failures Persist">
    If payment attempts continue to decline despite verbal confirmation from your bank:
    1. **Obtain Official Documentation**: Request written confirmation from your bank verifying that Tavly transactions are approved.
       * The statement must appear on official bank letterhead.
       * The document must explicitly state that transactions from Tavly are approved and unblocked.
    2. **Email Support**: Forward the confirmation document to [support@tavlyai.com](mailto:support@tavlyai.com).
    3. **Include Workspace Details**: Include your Tavly account email, workspace ID, and the transaction amount.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add an Alternative Payment Method">
    If your primary card issuer cannot process the transaction, configure a secondary payment method immediately:
    1. Navigate to the **Billing** tab in the Tavly dashboard.
    2. Click **Change payment methods** to access the secure Stripe billing interface.
    3. Click **Add payment method** and enter credentials for an alternate corporate credit card or debit card.
    4. Set the new card as your default payment method and reprocess pending charges. Review [Add payment methods](/accounts/add-payment) for a detailed walkthrough.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How do you prevent future payment declines and service interruptions?

Prevent future payment failures by configuring a **secondary backup payment method** in Stripe, maintaining a buffer in your **auto-recharge threshold**, and verifying that your corporate card permits **recurring automated international charges**. Regularly monitor billing notification emails to resolve card expirations before invoices fail.

Implement these proactive measures to ensure uninterrupted uptime:

* **Configure Generous Auto-Recharge Buffers**: Set your auto-recharge trigger threshold high enough (e.g., trigger at \$20, replenish to \$100) so high call volume spikes do not exhaust credits before a payment completes.
* **Maintain Updated Expiration Dates**: Review card expiration dates ahead of time; replace expiring corporate cards before end-of-month invoicing cycles.
* **Designate a Billing Admin**: Assign a dedicated billing contact within your organization to immediately receive and action automated decline notifications.

If you continue to experience billing issues after following these steps, contact our support team through the [Customer Support Portal](https://support.tavlyai.com/) or via email at [support@tavlyai.com](mailto:support@tavlyai.com) for direct engineering assistance.
