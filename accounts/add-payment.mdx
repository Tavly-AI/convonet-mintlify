## Why is adding a payment method required in Tavly AI?

Adding a **Stripe payment method** is required to purchase prepaid credits, unlock auto recharge, acquire dedicated phone numbers, and maintain active calling after exhausting the **\$10 free trial credit**. All transactions are processed through Stripe's certified **PCI-DSS Level 1 infrastructure** to guarantee payment security.

Adding a valid payment method ensures uninterrupted production availability for your conversational agents. Depending on your workspace configuration, the card on file serves specific transactional functions.

<Note>
  **Workspace-Level Payment Isolation**: Payment methods are scoped **per workspace**. Adding a credit card in one workspace does not carry it over to other workspaces under the same user login. Every workspace maintains its own independent billing setup, credit balance, usage totals, and invoice history. If you create a second workspace, switch into it and attach a payment method before running calls. See [Create and manage Tavly workspaces](/accounts/workspace) for multi-workspace switching.
</Note>

## How do you add or update a payment method in your workspace?

To add or update a payment method, open the **Billing tab** in the Tavly dashboard, click **Change payment methods** to launch the secure **Stripe Customer Portal**, and click **Add payment method**. Enter your card details and save; the card becomes active immediately for workspace charges.

## How is payment data isolated across multiple workspaces?

Payment methods are strictly isolated **per workspace**. Adding or modifying a credit card in one workspace does not link or apply it to any other workspaces under the same user credentials. Each project or enterprise workspace manages independent **payment cards**, **billing histories**, **prepaid balances**, and **monthly invoices**.

This isolation provides enterprise security and cost-center accounting separation:

* **Departmental Billing**: Development, Staging, and Production workspaces can be linked to separate corporate cards or accounting cost centers.
* **Client Account Segregation**: Agencies managing client voice agents can attach client-owned cards directly to dedicated client workspaces without cross-contamination.
* **Service Resiliency**: A payment dispute or card expiration in one workspace does not disrupt telephony routing or agents in sibling workspaces.
