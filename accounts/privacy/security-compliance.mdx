## What security certifications and compliance standards does Tavly AI uphold?

Tavly AI adheres to enterprise-grade privacy and security standards, holding active **SOC 2 Type 1 and Type 2 certifications**, **HIPAA compliance** for Protected Health Information (PHI), and **GDPR readiness**. Independent third-party auditors continuously verify Tavly's operational controls across data security, system availability, and customer confidentiality.

Ensuring customer data protection is fundamental to Tavly's engineering architecture. Enterprise workloads are deployed across isolated cloud infrastructure with AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 encryption in transit.

### Certifications and Compliance Matrix

| Standard / Framework | Regulatory Scope | Audit & Verification Status | Mandatory Prerequisite |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **HIPAA** | Protected Health Information (PHI) under US healthcare law. | Fully compliant infrastructure and organizational policies. | Requires a fully executed Business Associate Agreement (BAA). |
| **SOC 2 Type 1 & Type 2** | Security, Availability, and Confidentiality trust service criteria. | Independently audited and certified by an accredited AICPA firm. | Attestation reports available to enterprise teams upon NDA/request. |
| **GDPR** | EU & UK General Data Protection Regulation. | Compliant infrastructure hosted on AWS with an active DPA. | Requires execution of standard DPA including EU Standard Contractual Clauses. |

To review active certificates, independent audit reports, continuous monitoring telemetry, and our real-time security posture, visit the official [Compliance Trust Center](https://app.vanta.com/re-tell.ai/trust/8nfvavp5klt9n4iz32h90).

## How do you execute a BAA, DPA, or Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)?

To execute a **Business Associate Agreement (BAA)** or **Data Processing Addendum (DPA with EU SCCs)**, visit our self-signing legal portal at [click-agreements.retellai.com](https://click-agreements.retellai.com/). Agreements are completed electronically at **no additional cost** and bind your workspace to strict healthcare and privacy processing standards.

### Supported Self-Service Agreements

* **Business Associate Agreement (BAA)**: Legally mandated for healthcare providers, covered entities, and healthtech vendors before transmitting Protected Health Information (PHI) through Tavly conversational agents.
* **Data Processing Addendum (DPA) & Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)**: Mandated under Article 28 of GDPR for processing personal data belonging to European Union or United Kingdom residents.

### Recommended Agent Privacy Configurations After Execution

Once your legal agreements are executed, configure your agent workspaces to enforce least-privilege data storage:

1. **Configure Automated Expiration**: Set an automated deletion schedule (from 1 day up to 2 years) for all transcripts, recordings, and logs via the [Data Retention Policy](/accounts/privacy/data-retention).
2. **Enable PII Scrubbing**: Strip sensitive entities (SSNs, medical IDs, payment details, phone numbers) post-call using [Data Storage Settings](/accounts/privacy/data-storage).
3. **Enforce 24-Hour Link Expiration**: Restrict unauthorized media access by opting into [Secure URLs](/accounts/privacy/secure-url) for call recordings and execution logs.

## How does Tavly AI maintain GDPR compliance?

Tavly maintains GDPR compliance by hosting infrastructure on **Amazon Web Services (AWS)** under an enterprise Data Processing Addendum and offering self-service **Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)**. While Tavly's processing servers currently reside in United States cloud regions, all personal data handling adheres to strict EU data subject protections.

* **Infrastructure Safeguards**: Hosted exclusively within SOC 2 and ISO 27001 certified AWS data centers.
* **Data in Transit and at Rest**: Audio streams and API interactions are encrypted with TLS 1.3; database volumes, backups, and audio files are encrypted with AES-256.
* **Telemetry Privacy**: Tavly enforces strict telemetry filters that omit personal customer data from internal debugging logs and performance telemetry.

## How do you submit a GDPR personal data erasure request?

To exercise your **GDPR Right to Erasure**, first delete your workspace via [Account Deletion](/accounts/account#delete-your-account) to immediately purge calls, recordings, and agent configurations. Then, email **`support@tavlyai.com`** requesting permanent data erasure to wipe your profile from internal business tools and third-party CRMs.

Execute the following two-step procedure to complete full data erasure:

### Step 1: Self-Service Workspace Deletion

1. Log in to the Tavly dashboard as an account administrator.
2. Navigate to **Account Settings** and select [Delete your account](/accounts/account#delete-your-account).
3. Confirm deletion to permanently drop your workspace, phone numbers, custom voice models, conversation flows, and stored call histories.

### Step 2: Formal Erasure Notification

1. Send an email from your registered account address to [support@tavlyai.com](mailto:support@tavlyai.com) with the subject line: `GDPR Right to Erasure Request`.
2. Tavly's data protection team will permanently delete your identity records, email history, and customer profile across all integrated operational subprocessors (including CRM, customer support desks, and identity verification partners).
3. You will receive formal written confirmation upon completion.

<Note>
  **Permanent Deletion Notice**: Erasure requests are irreversible. Once processed, account records, agent configurations, and past conversation analytics cannot be restored.
</Note>
