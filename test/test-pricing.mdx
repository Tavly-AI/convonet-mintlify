Agent testing incurs charges at the **exact same rates as production traffic**. Tavly does not provide a separate disconnected sandbox or discounted test tier; compute consumed during testing is billed identically to live production workloads. Factor test runs into your resource planning, and consult the [Tavly pricing page](https://www.tavlyai.com/pricing) for real-time rates.

## How does agent testing pricing work?

**Agent testing pricing** mirrors live production traffic rates across all environments. Tavly does not operate a separate disconnected sandbox or offer discounted test rates; compute consumed during development is billed identically to live workloads, requiring teams to account for per-message text generation and per-minute voice runtime during testing.

Factor test runs into your resource planning, and consult the [Tavly pricing page](https://www.tavlyai.com/pricing) for real-time rates.

| Method | Metering Basis | Pricing Model |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

## How are text-based testing costs calculated?

**Text-based testing costs** are metered on a per-message basis across the LLM Playground, simulation testing, and batch suites based on the underlying language models. Rates typically range from **$0.001 to $0.05 per message**, influenced by model complexity, dual-model consumption in synthetic simulations, and automated post-call evaluation grading surcharges.

Consult the [pricing page](https://www.tavlyai.com/pricing) for granular per-model figures. Keep three compounding cost drivers in mind when budgeting text runs:

* **Dual-model consumption in simulations**: The **LLM setting** designated in a test case governs strictly the simulated caller persona. The agent's replies bill according to its dedicated [LLM settings](/build/llm-options). Pairing an inexpensive caller model with a high-tier agent model still incurs full charges for the agent's messages.
* **Multiplicative scaling across batches**: Batch runs evaluate each included case without bulk discounting. Running a 20-case test suite three consecutive times incurs charges for 60 individual simulation runs.
* **Evaluation grading fees**: Automated rubric evaluation adds one post-call analysis unit per case to score pass/fail outcomes. Unsaved manual chats in the playground do not execute evaluation rubrics and do not incur this surcharge.

## How are voice testing costs calculated?

**Voice testing costs** are billed on a per-minute basis combining voice streaming infrastructure, text-to-speech (**TTS**) audio synthesis, and LLM inference. Total rates generally span **$0.07 to $0.31 per minute**. Browser web calls avoid carrier transit fees, while phone tests incur additional carrier fees and monthly phone number leases.

Both [Web call testing](/test/test-web) and [phone call testing](/test/test-phone) stream live voice audio and bill on a **per-minute** basis identical to live production sessions:

* **Telephony surcharges on phone tests**: Inbound and outbound phone tests require an active [phone number](/deploy/purchase-number) ($2.00/month) and incur PSTN carrier fees (approximately $0.015 per minute on Tavly numbers).
* **Zero carrier fees on web calls**: Web call tests run in the browser via WebRTC, bypassing telephony carrier transit charges entirely.

Because voice minutes cost significantly more than text turns, perform the bulk of functional debugging using text simulation, reserving browser and telephony voice calls for final acoustic and conversational sign-off.

## What quotas and allowances apply to AI Agent and Conductor?

**AI Agent allocations** provide an included daily quota of **30 messages per user and 200 per workspace each day**, resetting at midnight Pacific Time. Usage beyond this allowance bills at **$0.20 per message** under pay-as-you-go billing, while simulated conversations run by the agent bill at standard simulation rates.

[AI Agent](/test/testing-with-conductor) provides an included daily quota: **30 messages per user and 200 per workspace each day**, resetting at midnight Pacific Time. Beyond this allotment, AI Agent bills at **\$0.20 per message**, provided a workspace administrator has enabled pay-as-you-go billing for the feature. If disabled, AI Agent pauses requests until the daily quota resets. Your account must also maintain active billing in good standing; expired trials or payment failures immediately disable access.

Simulated conversations executed by AI Agent on your behalf bill at standard per-message simulation rates. Review your detailed ledger under <code className="ui-btn">Billing → Usage</code>.

## What complimentary allowances are included?

Tavly provides several **complimentary allowances** to help developers prototype without immediate out-of-pocket expense. New accounts receive **$10 in initial free credit**, **20 free concurrent call channels**, daily quotas for AI Agent, and **100 complimentary minutes for AI QA** before standard per-minute billing applies.

Every workspace benefits from the following promotional baseline:

* **\$10 in free credit** applied upon account registration.
* **20 free concurrent call** channels.
* **AI Agent**: 30 complimentary messages per user and 200 per workspace daily (resetting at midnight PT).
* **AI QA**: The initial 100 minutes are complimentary, billing at \$0.10 per minute thereafter. See [AI QA](/ai-qa/overview).

With the exception of daily AI Agent messages, testing directly decrements your account credit balance. Note that leasing a [phone number](/deploy/purchase-number) requires a valid payment card on file, meaning [phone call testing](/test/test-phone) requires payment setup even if promotional signup credits remain available.
