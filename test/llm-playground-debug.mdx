When an agent produces unexpected phrasing, navigates to an incorrect branch, or provides erroneous arguments to a tool, the **Debug tool** allows you to isolate and resolve that specific turn directly rather than adjusting global prompts or conversational graphs blindly. Open the debugger on any response, state transition, or tool call inside the [LLM Playground](/test/llm-playground) to view recommended architectural remedies, then regenerate the turn to confirm resolution before live deployment.

## What is the response debugger in the LLM Playground?

The **response debugger** is an in-console diagnostic utility within the Tavly LLM Playground that isolates and resolves unexpected agent phrasing, erroneous tool arguments, or incorrect node transitions directly on specific turns. Rather than adjusting global prompts blindly, developers inspect tailored architectural remedies and verify behavioral consistency using iterative regenerations.

Debug functionality adapts dynamically to your agent's underlying architecture:

| Capability | Conversation Flow Agents | Single-Prompt & Multi-Prompt Agents |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Response Generation Debug** | Full remediation (examples, node splitting, temperature tuning) | Temperature tuning primary remedy |
| **Node Routing & Transition Analysis** | Dedicated transition debugging & distribution inspection | Not applicable (single graph context) |
| **Tool Invocation Debugging** | Parameter inspection & 10-trial sampling | Parameter inspection & 10-trial sampling |
| **10-Iteration Variance Sampling** | Available for answers, transitions, and tools | Available for answers and tools |

<Note>
  Debug functionality is embedded within the LLM Playground and adapts to your agent's underlying architecture. [Conversation Flow](/build/setup/conversation-flow/conversation-flow-overview) agents unlock complete debugging capabilities, including node routing analysis and node-level structural modifications. Single-prompt and multi-prompt agents offer response generation and tool execution debugging, highlighting model temperature tuning as the primary remedy.
</Note>

## How do you resolve incorrect responses?

To resolve an incorrect response, click the **Debug** button on the agent's utterance in the transcript to open targeted remediation options. Select from adding fine-tuning examples, splitting overloaded nodes, or adjusting LLM temperature, then run single or 10-answer regenerations to verify that phrasing and tone align with operational guidelines.

Follow these structured steps to remediate flawed agent utterances:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Debug on the response">
    Click the <code className="ui-btn">Debug</code> button attached to the agent's utterance in the transcript.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review recommended fixes">
    The <code className="ui-btn">Debug the AI response</code> modal displays remediation strategies tailored to the issue, linking directly to step-by-step documentation:

    * [Add Fine-tuning Examples](/build/setup/conversation-flow/debug-guide#how-do-you-fix-an-agent-ignoring-instructions) to teach desired tone, format, and syntax.
    * [Split One Node into Two Nodes](/build/setup/conversation-flow/debug-guide#how-do-you-fix-broken-node-transitions) when an individual node handles too many conversational objectives.
    * [Adjust LLM Temperature](/build/setup/conversation-flow/debug-guide#how-do-you-fix-an-agent-ignoring-instructions) to reduce generation variance in favor of strict predictability.

    For single-prompt and multi-prompt agents, temperature tuning is the sole suggested fix because these architectures do not use discrete graph nodes.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Regenerate to verify">
    Select <code className="ui-btn">Regenerate the answer</code> to generate a single response reflecting your configuration adjustments. Use <code className="ui-btn">Regenerate 10 answers</code> to sample a 10-turn frequency distribution and confirm consistency across multiple inferences.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How do you resolve transition and routing errors?

To fix transition and routing errors, click **Debug** on the transition indicator pill or select **Didn't transition as expected?** on the agent utterance. Review tailored remedies—such as providing explicit fine-tuning transition examples or splitting competing criteria across nodes—and evaluate stability by regenerating 10 transitions to inspect destination node distributions.

When an agent shifts to an incorrect node or fails to transition when criteria are met:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Debug on the transition">
    Click <code className="ui-btn">Debug</code> on the agent response and select <code className="ui-btn">Didn't transition as expected?</code>, or click <code className="ui-btn">Debug</code> directly on the transition indicator pill in the transcript.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review recommended transition fixes">
    Follow the linked guides to refine transition logic:

    * [Add Fine-tuning Transition Examples](/build/setup/conversation-flow/advanced/finetune-examples#when-to-use-transition-examples) to provide explicit routing patterns for ambiguous inputs.
    * [Split One Node into Two Nodes](/build/setup/conversation-flow/debug-guide#how-do-you-fix-broken-node-transitions) to cleanly decouple competing routing criteria.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Regenerate the transition">
    Click <code className="ui-btn">Regenerate the transition</code> to re-evaluate routing with your modified rules, or choose <code className="ui-btn">Regenerate 10 transitions</code> to check the destination node distribution across 10 separate evaluations.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How do you resolve tool and function call issues?

To resolve tool and function call issues, hover over the **Tool Invocation** pill in the conversation transcript and click **Debug**. The diagnostic modal reveals misconfigured arguments or incorrect tool triggers, allowing you to test individual invocations or sample parameter stability across **10 trial executions** under identical conversational contexts.

When an agent invokes the wrong external function or passes invalid parameters:

* **Inspect invocation payloads**: Hover over the corresponding <code className="ui-btn">Tool Invocation</code> entry in the conversation transcript and click <code className="ui-btn">Debug</code>.
* **Review configuration guidelines**: The <code className="ui-btn">Debug the tool invocation</code> interface outlines applicable schema and prompt corrections to ensure arguments adhere to expected types and ranges.
* **Verify single executions**: Use <code className="ui-btn">Regenerate the tool invocation</code> to test a single invocation against the latest settings.
* **Evaluate argument stability**: Select <code className="ui-btn">Regenerate 10 tool invocations</code> to inspect parameter stability across 10 independent trials without modifying external production state.

## How do you manage response consistency and output variance?

Manage response consistency by analyzing non-deterministic turns with the **10-iteration verification tool** and applying targeted stabilization techniques. Running 10 consecutive trials tabulates an exact frequency distribution of outcomes, helping developers identify dominant probabilistic paths and confirm that temperature tuning, node decomposition, or fine-tuning examples successfully eliminate erratic output variations.

If identical user inputs yield inconsistent agent outputs, click <code className="ui-btn">Debug</code> on the target turn to implement core stabilization strategies:

* **[Add fine-tuning examples](/build/setup/conversation-flow/debug-guide#how-do-you-fix-an-agent-ignoring-instructions)**: Anchor intended phrasing and establish clear syntactic constraints for edge-case prompts.
* **[Split the node](/build/setup/conversation-flow/debug-guide#how-do-you-fix-broken-node-transitions)**: Reduce prompt complexity by decomposing nodes with conflicting or overloaded instructions into discrete single-purpose states.
* **[Adjust the temperature](/build/setup/conversation-flow/debug-guide#how-do-you-fix-an-agent-ignoring-instructions)**: Lower model temperature toward `0.0` to favor deterministic generations over probabilistic variance.

Every debug panel includes dedicated 10-iteration verification actions:

| Sampling Action | Target Evaluated | Statistical Output Tabulated |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| <code className="ui-btn">Regenerate 10 answers</code> | Conversational utterance text | Frequency distribution of distinct phrasing variants |
| <code className="ui-btn">Regenerate 10 transitions</code> | Node routing decisions | Frequency count of destination state nodes |
| <code className="ui-btn">Regenerate 10 tool invocations</code> | External tool trigger & payload | Parameter consistency count and schema conformity |

Each sampling action executes 10 times consecutively under identical prompt inputs and tabulates distinct outcomes with their exact frequency counts out of 10. This identifies dominant paths on non-deterministic turns and confirms that your changes deliver predictable behavior.
