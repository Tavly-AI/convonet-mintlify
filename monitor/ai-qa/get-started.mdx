## What is Tavly AI QA and how do you access the dashboard?

**Tavly AI Quality Assurance (AI QA)** is accessed directly through the primary dashboard navigation sidebar. Clicking the **AI Quality Assurance** tab opens the centralized supervision console, providing administrators and engineering teams with immediate visibility into active evaluation cohorts, historical audit benchmarks, resolution trends, and direct tools to launch new automated call assessments.

### Navigation and Prerequisites

1. **Dashboard Navigation**: Log in to your Tavly workspace and locate **AI Quality Assurance** in the primary navigation menu.
2. **Console State**: If cohorts already exist in your workspace, the dashboard displays active summaries, average quality scores, and recent evaluations. If no cohorts exist, an onboarding screen prompts you to create your initial evaluation.
3. **Role Access**: Ensure your account holds **Admin**, **Developer**, or custom analytics write permissions to initialize evaluations.

## How do you launch a new automated call quality evaluation?

To **launch a new automated call evaluation**, navigate to the top-right corner of the AI QA console and click **Create QA**. This action initiates the interactive evaluation wizard, prompting you to configure target cohort parameters, set sampling criteria, establish resolution benchmarks, and schedule automated evaluations across voice conversations.

### What Occurs Upon Initialization

Clicking **Create QA** opens the structured cohort builder:
* **Cohort Scoping**: Select the specific conversational agents and date intervals to evaluate.
* **Sampling Distribution**: Designate whether to audit 100% of conversations or a statistically significant sample percentage (e.g., 10%–25%).
* **Rule Assignment**: Link custom resolution rules and scoring thresholds to the cohort.

## What steps follow cohort creation in the evaluation pipeline?

Following initial creation, the **AI QA evaluation pipeline** guides you through three essential milestones: defining a targeted cohort by filtering specific agents and date ranges, establishing custom resolution criteria and latency thresholds, and inspecting transcript-backed diagnostic reports in the analytics dashboard to resolve performance bottlenecks.
